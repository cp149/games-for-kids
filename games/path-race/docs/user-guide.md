# Path Race - User Guide

> Learn how to play Path Race and beat the AI opponent!

## 🎮 How to Play

### Game Objective

**Race against the AI to create a valid path from the green dot to the red dot, visiting every dot exactly once!**

### Basic Rules

1. **🟢 Start at Green** - Click the green dot to begin your path
2. **⬜ Visit All Dots** - Every white dot must be visited exactly once
3. **↕️ Move Orthogonally** - You can only move up, down, left, or right (no diagonal moves)
4. **🔴 End at Red** - Your path must finish at the red dot
5. **🏁 Beat the AI** - Complete your path before the AI to win!

### Controls

#### Desktop
- **Click**: Click on dots to add them to your path
- **Undo Button** ↶: Remove the last dot from your path
- **Restart** 🔄: Start the current level over
- **Home** 🏠: Return to the main menu
- **Music** 🔊: Toggle background music on/off
- **Language** 🌐: Switch between English, 中文, 日本語

#### Mobile/Tablet
- **Tap**: Tap on dots to select them
- All buttons are large and touch-friendly!

#### Keyboard Shortcuts
- **U**: Undo last move
- **R**: Restart level
- **Escape**: Go to home

## 🤖 Understanding the AI

### What is the AI Doing?

The AI opponent uses a smart algorithm called **Ant Colony Optimization** (ACO). Watch the AI side to see how it works:

1. **Exploration Phase**: Little "ants" explore different paths
2. **Learning Phase**: The AI learns which paths are better
3. **Solution Phase**: The AI executes its best path

### Visual Feedback

- **Orange Dots**: Dots the AI has visited
- **Orange Lines**: The AI's path
- **Glowing Trails**: "Pheromone" trails left by exploring ants
- **Progress Bar**: How close the AI is to finishing

## 💡 Tips & Strategies

### For Beginners

1. **Take Your Time**: There's no penalty for thinking!
2. **Use Undo**: Made a mistake? Just undo and try again
3. **Watch the AI**: See how it solves the puzzle
4. **Plan Ahead**: Think 2-3 moves in advance

### Advanced Strategies

1. **Look for Patterns**: Some grid layouts have common solutions
2. **Avoid Dead Ends**: Make sure you don't trap yourself
3. **Corner Strategy**: Work around the edges first
4. **Speed Practice**: Once you know the solution, try for 3 stars!

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Clicking Too Fast**: Think before you click!
❌ **Ignoring Corners**: Corner dots can be tricky
❌ **Not Using Undo**: It's there to help you learn
❌ **Giving Up**: Every puzzle is solvable!

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: What if I make a mistake?

**A**: Just click the Undo button (↶) to remove your last move. You can undo as many times as you want!

### Q: How does the AI work?

**A**: The AI uses Ant Colony Optimization, inspired by how real ants find food. It explores different paths and learns which ones are best!

### Q: Can I play offline?

**A**: Yes! Once you've loaded the game once, it works without internet.

### Q: Is there a time limit?

**A**: No hard time limit, but you need to beat the AI to get 2-3 stars!

### Q: What happens if the AI wins?

**A**: You can still finish the puzzle to practice, and you'll get 1 star. Then you can retry for a better score!

### Q: Can I skip a level?

**A**: No, you need to complete each level with at least 1 star to unlock the next one.

### Q: Are all puzzles solvable?

**A**: Yes! Every puzzle is guaranteed to have at least one solution.

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

### Game won't load
- Make sure you're using a modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
- Clear your browser cache
- Disable browser extensions temporarily

### Dots won't click
- Make sure you're clicking an adjacent dot (up/down/left/right only)
- Check that the dot hasn't been visited already
- Try the undo button to reset your path

### Audio not playing
- Check your device volume
- Make sure browser isn't muted
- Try clicking the 🔊 button to toggle music

### Progress not saving
- Make sure browser cookies/localStorage is enabled
- Don't use incognito/private mode
- Progress is saved locally (not in the cloud)
