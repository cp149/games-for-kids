🎮 Extension Showcase

Demonstrates all 4 extensions working together:

✅ SimpleI18n - Multi-language (1/2/3 keys)
✅ ScoreManager - Scoring & combos
✅ TextToSpeech - Voice feedback (T to toggle)
✅ UIComponents - Buttons, modals, stars

Controls: Click coins to collect | T = Toggle TTS | 1/2/3 = Languages
